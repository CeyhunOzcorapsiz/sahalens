# Research roadmap

These are development priorities, not claims of completed functionality.

1. **Real-broadcast accuracy:** Measure event-level precision and recall and calibrate thresholds across camera and broadcast formats.
2. **Team and player identity:** Improve team assignment, long-term tracking and identity fragmentation.
3. **Pitch calibration:** Improve metric coordinates under camera movement and panoramic views.
4. **Transparent reporting:** Explain data sufficiency, uncertainty and the distinction between sample and measured results.
5. **Coach experience:** Reduce review time with useful clip selection, clear reports and a focused meeting workflow.
6. **Maintainability:** Maintain automated checks, modular components and reproducible model evaluations.
7. **English interface:** Add shared translations for navigation, dynamic messages, dialogs and exported reports. The current application interface is Turkish.
